Aurin Basecamp

Aurin Basecamp is a research facility built upon and around a wheat field located 20 minutes southwest of Nocturne City. The facility's primary research objective of Aurin Basecamp regards molecular photodissociation. Its construction was spurred by the discovery of a cross-dimensional gateway found within the field. Research expeditions have been sent out through the gateway, and numerous lifeforms have been brought back to be studied, including sentient species. Upon entry, subjects are decontaminated, supplied with various vaccines, and watched for body composition changes over two months until being moved to another section of the facility. Heather Ouras, lacking a surname at the time of her intake to Aurin, was the first documented rescue. Upon entering the vicinity of the active gateway, their body began changing composition. While previously slightly amorphous, exposure to a decontamination bay in the unknown alternate world had resulted in her body taking a permanent shape. Aurin's research team is comprised of a division of Cero Zentali, a company formally known for the development of radio-signal based technologies. The division was formed on 7/20/2022, where it promptly began investigating anomalies in seismological and electrical data.

[TieR3 Gate]

The Tier3 Gate is operated by ionizing a high amount of gas fixed within a round frame. During this process, molecular bonds are prevented from being created or destroyed, until a target charge is reached, upon which the machine's stability mechanism is disabled. The charge overshoots nearby matter, puncturing a localized hole within spacetime. Persons that choose to flee the wasteland on the other side undergo physical changes soon after crossing the portal.
