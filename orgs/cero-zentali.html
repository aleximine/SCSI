Cero Zentali

Cero Zentali is a global producer of radio and wireless communication parts and supplies. Originally a government-funded corporation, it provided the military of ████████ with radio equipment ██████████████████. Cero Zentali, as an electronics supplier, now fronts for the detection, protection, and prevention of thin points in spacetime across the globe. However, it is still restricted to the scope of Earth - and as such, distant planets are still susceptible to collapse.
