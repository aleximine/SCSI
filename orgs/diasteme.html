DIASTEME

DIASTEME. Founded as Global and Interstellar Accessors (GaIA), DIASTEME as a conglomerate company controls the flow of space travel to and from Earth. By 2025, the world is on the early stages of viable hovertechnology; a number of eager hobbyists even start building their own cruise shuttles. In response to the growing field of space engineering, GaIA is founded as a startup to attempt regulation of individuals entering High Earth Orbit, preventing colonization of other planets by individuals. Its primary stations function as a blockade across Earth, where proper clearance is required to properly leave the planet. As GaIA grew in size, it started to focus on producing mining and cargo shuttles under the subsidary DIASTEME, which later became the name of the conglomerate.
